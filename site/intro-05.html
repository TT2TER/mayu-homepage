About

I study financial market microstructure, high-frequency and intraday trading, and trading agents, as well as token communication and semantic communication under bandwidth and latency constraints.

I began my doctoral studies in September 2025. At Beijing Institute of Technology, I am advised by Prof. Jun Zhang, a member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, and Prof. Zhen Gao on token communication and semantic communication. At Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, I am advised by Yitong Duan on AI for finance and agents. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology.

Research

AI for Finance

I study financial market microstructure and AI methods for high-frequency and intraday trading, with an interest in market simulation and trading agents.

  • Market modeling. Exploring large models for financial market dynamics and high-frequency and intraday trading.
  • Market simulation and trading agents. Studying market dynamics at the microstructure level and agents that interact with financial markets.

Token (Semantic) Communication

I explore token communication and semantic communication for multimodal information under challenging channel conditions, with an interest in meeting low-latency communication requirements.

  • Token communication. Large-model-enabled unified multimodal transmission for 6G connectivity across people, devices, and intelligent systems, under low signal-to-noise ratios, limited bit budgets, and latency constraints.
  • Efficient inference. Model compression and inference acceleration for low-latency communication.
